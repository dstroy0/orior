\chapter{\texorpdfstring{Engine file types}{Engine file types}}
\label{chap:file-types-table}

Every file extension the engine and its projects write or read. A new extension is Doug's to name. Before one is proposed, it is checked against this table and against the whole biohub tree, orior\_\allowbreak{}python included.

\texttt{.\allowbreak{}kcr} & Kolmogorov information crystal: its own compression format. It reads every source format, takes each format's own compression out, and can rebuild the data into any format (Doug). krep kind “KCR\textbackslash{}0”, version 1. The only crystal format. & Doug \\
\texttt{.\allowbreak{}krs} & Kolmogorov information ruleset: one language's forms (\texttt{ptx.\allowbreak{}krs}, \texttt{c.\allowbreak{}krs}, \texttt{vhdl.\allowbreak{}krs}). & Doug \\
\texttt{.\allowbreak{}kcs} & Kolmogorov information construction set: what reconstructs information. A target's forms and their costs are its construction set. krep kind “KCS\textbackslash{}0”. & Doug \\
\texttt{.\allowbreak{}knf} & Kolmogorov noise floor. A sample's floor is its entropy history. krep kind “KNF\textbackslash{}0”. & Doug \\
\texttt{.\allowbreak{}kdm} & Kolmogorov device map: the hardware map. & Doug \\
\texttt{.\allowbreak{}ksc} & Kolmogorov system classification: the language map. & Doug \\
\texttt{.\allowbreak{}kqr} & Kolmogorov query record: every ask put to a member and what came back, then the paths read off those asks (\texttt{src/\allowbreak{}cu/\allowbreak{}types/\allowbreak{}file\_\allowbreak{}defs/\allowbreak{}kqr}). & Doug \\
\texttt{.\allowbreak{}khw} & Kolmogorov hardware: the part's instructions as the part answered them, each form, its encoding and its operands' fields (\texttt{src/\allowbreak{}cu/\allowbreak{}types/\allowbreak{}file\_\allowbreak{}defs/\allowbreak{}khw}). & Doug \\
