
\chapter{The eye test, and the null known in advance}

\textbf{Purpose:} Ask whether a line integral through the compression state predicts the final
digest where a region integral could not, and then establish that the reach of any such null is
derivable before the measurement is taken. \textbf{Scope:} \texttt{beam\_read.py},
\texttt{build\_beam\_view.py}, \texttt{null\_first.py}, \texttt{aimed\_arms.py}.

\section{Why this ran when the question was already answered}

\texttt{nonce\_read.py} asked the same question and returned null, and that null is real. But it was
measured with six scalars: weight, monopole share, dipole share, quadrupole share, fine-structure
share, and the zonal dipole. \textbf{Five of those are power shares, and a power share is invariant
under rotation by construction.} An instrument assembled from rotation invariants cannot see
orientation at any strength.

The state carries 256 numbers, one per bit, and that is the rank bound this tree has now measured
five separate ways. So the prior null covered a six-dimensional rotation-averaged summary of a
256-dimensional object. It is a real result about that summary and it was never a result about the
object.

\section{The instrument}

A harmonic row is a whole-sphere integral. An arm is a region integral. A \textbf{beam is a line
integral}: occlusion along a ray, weighted by perpendicular distance to it. It reads passively:
it observes from outside, and does not perturb what it observes.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lr}
\toprule
map & rank \\
\midrule
harmonic rows, degree 8 & 81 \\
beam rows, 256 line integrals & 256 \\
both stacked & 256 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The beams add \textbf{175 dimensions of rank} the region reading does not have. They are a different
row type and not a restatement of one.

the 95th percentile of the largest absolute correlation over the same feature count under 200
shuffled pairings.

\section{The free exact null, which is ala control}

The nonce sits in schedule word 15, and round \(r\) consumes schedule word \(r\). So rounds 0 through
14 consume words 0 through 14, every one identical across the sweep, and \textbf{states 0 through 15
are bit-identical for every nonce}. State 16 is the first the nonce has reached.

Rounds 8 and 15 are therefore an exact null by construction and they are in the table for that
reason: they must read exactly \(0.0000\), and variance there is a wiring bug, and not a
discovery. Verified at 192, 256 and 500 nonces. An unlabelled zero in a results table reads as the
strongest measurement on it, and these therefore carry labels.

\section{Where the eyes do win}

On the one round carrying a signal, the two instruments do not read the same amount of it: beam
\(0.5361\) against harmonic \(0.2570\), a factor of \(\mathbf{2.09}\) on the same state, the same
target and the same null. That is the directional information a power share averages away, and it is
a measured property of the row type, and not a claim about it.

\textbf{It does not help a miner.} The early rounds have nothing for either instrument to find, and a
better instrument pointed at an empty room still reads empty.

\section{The null known in advance: the demon's position}

Every null above was earned the expensive way: run the instrument, fade an injected signal until it
disappears, report the level at which it vanished. That is honest and it is a measurement of the
instrument's blindness discovered by experiment.

\textbf{The kernel does not have to be discovered.} It is a property of the reading map
alone. Rank-nullity gives its dimension before any object is chosen, a singular value decomposition
gives an explicit basis for it, and every vector in that basis \emph{is} a null permutation: a
change to the object the reading cannot see, exactly, by construction, and not below a threshold.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrl}
\toprule
instrument & rank & kernel & a null from it excludes \\
\midrule
harmonic degree 2 & 9 & 247 & 3.5\% of directions \\
harmonic degree 4 & 25 & 231 & 9.8\% \\
harmonic degree 8 & 81 & 175 & 31.6\% \\
harmonic degree 15 & 256 & \textbf{0} & \textbf{the whole source space} \\
256 beams & 256 & \textbf{0} & \textbf{the whole source space} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Verified exactly: twelve kernel vectors derived from the map's decomposition, added to a random
object, moved the reading by \(7.1054 \times 10^{-15}\) against a reading scale of
\(3.4133 \times 10^{1}\), a relative change of \(2 \times 10^{-16}\), while the object itself moved
by \(0.678\).

This grades the two nulls in this research paper against each other. \texttt{nonce\_read.py} uses six scalars,
at most rank six of 256. Its null therefore excludes \textbf{at most 2.3\%} of the directions the state can
move in. \texttt{beam\_read.py} uses 256 beams, rank 256 and kernel 0, and its null therefore excludes
signal in \textbf{every} direction the object has. It is worth a run for that reason, and the reach is a
quantity, and not an assertion.

\section{The ceiling is the source count, not a wall}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{rrrrr}
\toprule
sources & rank & ceiling & kernel & arms to close \\
\midrule
64 & 64 & 64 & \textbf{0} & 0 \\
128 & 81 & 128 & 47 & 47 \\
256 & 81 & 256 & 175 & 175 \\
512 & 81 & 512 & 431 & 431 \\
1024 & 81 & 1024 & 943 & 943 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Arms to closure equals the kernel exactly at every width, and arms aimed at the kernel beat randomly
scattered arms by a factor of 1.00 to 1.02. \textbf{Position is free and the count is the bill.} At
64 sources a degree-8 reading has no kernel at all. Blindness appears only when the object carries
more sources than the reading has coefficients.

Two bounds in an earlier draft of this apparatus were the author's own and both were removed: an arm
cap of 1200, and a source count of 256 treated as fixed. A rank ceiling of 256 is a statement about a
256-source object and says nothing about arms.
